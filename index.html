Welcome to Small Brave Things

This is a creative wellness journal that helps you turn hard days into stories, tiny wins, and reminders to keep going.

What this app can do:
  • Help you write a daily reflection.
  • Turn your entry into a story, letter, old memory reflection, dream reflection, or book seed.
  • Save purpose cards and old entries so you can come back to them.
  • Give you a gentle next step when you feel stuck.
Important safety note:

This app is not therapy, medical care, or a crisis service. If you might hurt yourself or feel like you cannot stay safe, call or text 988, or use https://988lifeline.org/chat/.

Privacy note:

Your saved entries stay in this browser on this device unless you export a backup or copy text somewhere else.

You can open this again anytime from the Safety & Support section.
